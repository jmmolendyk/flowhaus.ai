02 Govern it

Custom Governance Frameworks

A repeatable oversight loop aligned to the NIST AI RMF and built into your existing governance, right-sized to your organization. Not a parallel bureaucracy.

Why it matters

Policies say what should happen. A framework is how it actually happens: who decides, what gets reviewed, when, and what evidence is kept. The NIST AI Risk Management Framework is a strong starting structure, but it is voluntary and deliberately non-prescriptive, so it has to be shaped to fit.

4core functions in the NIST AI RMF: Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage
19categories in the framework core, each with its own outcomes
72subcategories of outcomes to tailor to your organization

Source: NIST, AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (released January 2023; counts per published summaries of the core)

The point is fit. A fifty-person firm and a regional bank need different weight on the same loop. I build on the committees, review boards, and risk processes you already run, so AI oversight rides on existing habits rather than adding new ones.

What goes into it

  1. Map what exists.Review current governance, risk, privacy, security, and change processes to find what can be reused.
  2. Design the loop.Define roles, decision rights, review points, and escalation, using the NIST functions as the backbone.
  3. Right-size it.Match the depth of oversight to your risk: light for low-risk uses, rigorous for high-stakes ones.
  4. Pilot on one system.Run the framework on a real system, learn what works, and adjust.
  5. Expand gradually.Extend to further systems and teams once the pilot shows the loop holds.

What you have at the end
