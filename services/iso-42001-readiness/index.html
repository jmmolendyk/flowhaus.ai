03 Prove it

ISO/IEC 42001 Readiness & Implementation

A practical path to ISO/IEC 42001 certification, led by a BSI-certified Lead Implementer. ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for AI management systems.

Why it matters

Certification gives customers, partners, and regulators independent evidence that you manage AI deliberately. The standard certifies a management system, not a model or a product. It asks whether you have the policies, roles, risk processes, and controls to manage AI over time, and whether they work in practice.

I help you build and run the management system and get ready for the audit. I'm not a certification body and can't certify you; an accredited body does that independently. That independence is what gives the certificate its value.

What goes into it

  1. Scope and gap assessment.Define the scope of the AI management system and compare current practice against the standard.
  2. Build the management system.Put in place the policy, roles, risk and impact assessment processes, and applicable Annex A controls, sized to your organization.
  3. Operate and gather evidence.Run the system long enough to produce the records an auditor will ask to see.
  4. Internal audit and management review.Test the system yourselves first, and have leadership review it formally.
  5. Prepare for the certification audit.Coach your team through Stage 1 and Stage 2 with your chosen certification body, then plan for surveillance audits.
