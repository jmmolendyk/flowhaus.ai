Approach

Right-sized, practical, and learned in pilots.

  1. Right-size it

    Match governance to your size, risk, and maturity. Not a canned program.

  2. Meet you where you are

    Governance fits your current tools, processes, and pace. Work changes only where it helps.

  3. Start with a problem

    Build governance around one real use case, then expand outward by risk severity.

  4. Learn through pilots

    Expand based on what you learn, not on a master plan.

  5. Governance that moves

    It's living, actionable at the right levels and times, and the business doesn't stop for it.
